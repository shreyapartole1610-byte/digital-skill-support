📖 About Our Project

The Digital Skill Support for Local Government Office project aims to improve the digital knowledge and confidence of employees working in local government offices.

🎯 Project Aim

To improve digital skills and promote safe use of computers, internet services and digital tools.

📌 Objectives

Provide basic computer knowledge, digital documentation skills, online service awareness and cybersecurity guidance.

🌟 Benefits

Better productivity, easier digital communication, improved online safety and efficient office work.

💻 Digital Skills

Essential digital skills for effective work in local government offices.

💻 Computer Basics

Learn basic computer operations, files, folders and device management.

📄 Digital Documentation

Create, edit, save and manage official digital documents efficiently.

🌐 Internet Skills

Use internet services and online government portals safely and effectively.

📧 Email Skills

Learn how to send, receive and manage professional emails and attachments.

🔐 Cyber Safety

Follow safe practices while using computers, internet and online services.

🔑 Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords and avoid sharing passwords with others.

⚠️ Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown links or download files from untrusted sources.

🛡️ Protect Personal Data

Keep personal and official information safe while using online services.
