📖 About Our Project

The Digital Skill Support for Local Government Office project aims to improve the digital knowledge and confidence of employees working in local government offices.

🎯 Project Aim

To improve digital skills and promote safe use of computers, internet services and digital tools.

📌 Objectives

Provide basic computer knowledge, digital documentation skills, online service awareness and cybersecurity guidance.

🌟 Benefits

Better productivity, easier digital communication, improved online safety and efficient office work.

💻 Digital Skills

Essential digital skills for effective work in local government offices.

💻 Computer Basics

Learn basic computer operations, files, folders and device management.

📄 Digital Documentation

Create, edit, save and manage official digital documents efficiently.

🌐 Internet Skills

Use internet services and online government portals safely and effectively.

📧 Email Skills

Use email for official communication and sharing important documents.

🔐 Cybersecurity

Protect passwords, accounts and confidential government information.

📱 Digital Payments

Understand safe and responsible use of digital payment methods.

🔐 Cyber Safety

Follow these simple safety practices to protect official data, accounts and devices.

🔑 Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords for official accounts.

📱 Two-Factor Authentication

Enable 2FA to add an extra layer of account security.

🚫 Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown links or open suspicious attachments.

🔄 Keep Software Updated

Regularly update operating systems, apps and security software.

💾 Backup Important Data

Keep regular backups of important office documents and data.

🔒 Protect Confidential Data

Never share passwords, OTPs or sensitive government information.
